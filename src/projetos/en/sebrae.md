# Sebrae Ingresse

## Digital Education Hub for Small Businesses

Sebrae Ingresse is Sebrae's hub of digital educational solutions, aimed at micro business managers and individual micro-entrepreneurs. The platform brings together courses, learning journeys, mentoring, live activities with experts and networking among entrepreneurs, with access from computer and phone and digital certificate issuance.

The goal is to go beyond the traditional distance-learning format: content is organized into journeys for different entrepreneur profiles and business stages, classes have limited seats to encourage interaction, and live sessions gather dozens of participants at the same time. In 2025 there were 18 courses across 1,819 classes, averaging 65 enrollments per class, in programs such as Up Digital Marketing, Up Digital Finance, Decola MEI and the track for women entrepreneurs.

## Main features:

- **Courses and journeys:** Course catalog organized into learning journeys, with student progression and digital certification

- **Live classes:** Streaming with multiple participants, real-time interaction with experts and screen sharing

- **Admin panel:** Course creation, class opening, lesson scheduling, instructor management and enrollment and completion reports

- **User management:** Access control, permissions and tracking of student progress
