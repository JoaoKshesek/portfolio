# Loteria Nova

## Lottery Betting Intermediation Platform

Web system developed to make lottery betting intermediation and management easier. The platform offers an intuitive interface for users to place bets securely and conveniently, with full processing and validation integration.

## Main contributions:

- **Interface redesign:** Development and implementation of a new visual design, improving the platform's user experience and usability

- **Administrative features:** Maintenance and improvement of the administrative management systems, including bet control, reports and user management

- **Ticket printing system:** Creation of a robust system to generate and print betting tickets, with validation standards compatible with automatic readers, ensuring the integrity and traceability of every transaction
