# Busca Global

## Business Search and Promotion Platform

Web platform that connects those who search with those who offer: companies and professionals register their businesses and the public finds everything in one place, with simple search and navigation.

Project built **from scratch**, from the data model to the interface, with an admin panel to manage listings and platform content.

## Main contributions:

- **End-to-end build:** Full development of the platform in Next.js with a backend in Node and PHP/Laravel on MySQL

- **Search and navigation:** Implementation of the search and listing pages that are the heart of the product

- **Management panel:** Admin system to manage listings, content and the platform's operation

- **Infrastructure:** Application containerized with Docker, ready for deploy and scale
