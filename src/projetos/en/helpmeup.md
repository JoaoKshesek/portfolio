# Help Me Up

## Home Services Marketplace

Help Me Up is a platform that connects people who need a service to qualified providers nearby. The client picks the service, date and time, and the request is sent to providers within a 5 km radius. Payment is made by card inside the app, and every job can be rated, which builds the provider's reputation.

The services cover everyday needs of homes and small businesses: residential and commercial cleaning, window and glass cleaning, babysitting, dog walking and pet care, plant care during trips, gardening and key management. Providers go through background, experience and reference checks before joining the platform.

The product's pillars are safety, speed and ease: monitoring during the service, fast dispatch of the request, and a simple hiring, payment and receiving experience, with the support team responsible for resolving disputes.

## Main contributions:

- **App from scratch:** Development of the React Native app for iOS and Android, from architecture and navigation to the client and provider screens

- **Hiring flow:** Implementation of service, date and time selection, proximity-based request dispatch and provider acceptance

- **Payment and ratings:** Integration of credit and debit card payment and of the job rating system

- **Sign-up and verification:** Provider and client sign-up flows, with profile and document verification steps

- **Backend integration:** Consumption of the Node and PHP/Laravel APIs, with consistent error handling and loading states
