# Way Odontologia

## Clinical Decision Support Platform for Dentists

Way is a subscription platform made by dentists for dentists: it gathers, in one place, tools that help dentistry professionals and students make clinical decisions with more confidence. It is available as an iOS and Android app and also on the web, with the same account.

The platform's modules cover the daily routine of the practice:

- **Decision tree:** guided diagnosis by specialty, from restorative dentistry and endodontics to orthodontics and oral rehabilitation
- **New patient:** interactive anamnesis and physical exam script, with PDF generation
- **Technical guide and pharmacological manual:** technical-scientific library and drug descriptions with dosage and interactions
- **Complementary content, forum and calendar:** management and entrepreneurship material, community and appointment organization

## Main contributions:

- **Mobile app:** Development of the React Native app for iOS and Android, with navigation between modules and comfortable reading of long technical content

- **Web backoffice:** Construction of the React admin panel to manage subscribers, plans and the publication of clinical and pharmacological content

- **Interactive flows:** Implementation of the decision tree and the anamnesis script, with saved intermediate states and PDF export

- **Subscription and access:** Integration of subscription plans with module access control, in the app and on the web
